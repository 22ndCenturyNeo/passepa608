Privacy Policy

Effective October 9, 2026 · Developer: Myong-soo Lee

Pass EPA 608 does not collect any data about you.

The app works entirely on your iPhone. It has no account, no ads, no analytics, and it does not connect to the internet.

Information we collect

None. Pass EPA 608 does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information. It does not use advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, or tracking tools, and it contains no third-party SDKs.

Information stored on your device

To track your studying, the app saves the following only on your device:

  • Which questions you answered and whether you got them right
  • Missed questions and bookmarks
  • Mock exam scores and history
  • Your app settings

This information never leaves your device through the app. It may be included in your device backups (iCloud or computer), which Apple manages under its own privacy policy. You can delete it at any time in Settings → Reset all progress, or by deleting the app.

Network access

The app does not make network requests. All questions and explanations are built into the app.

Children

Because the app collects no information from anyone, it collects no information from children under 13.

Apple

Downloads and App Store activity are handled by Apple and covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
