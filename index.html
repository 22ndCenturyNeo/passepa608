Frequently asked questions

How do the mock exams work?

Each section has 25 questions, just like the real exam. Answers are graded when you submit, and you need 70% (18 of 25) in each section to pass. The Universal mock exam covers all four sections (100 questions).

What is Smart practice?

Smart practice picks 20 questions and weights them toward the topics and questions you miss most, while still covering every topic. Questions you recently answered correctly come up less often.

Where can I see my weak topics?

Your weakest topics appear on the home screen and in each section. Tap All topics in a section to see your accuracy for every topic, and tap any topic to drill 15 questions on it.

Does my progress sync between devices?

No. Your progress is stored only on your iPhone. It is included in your device backups, so restoring a backup restores your progress.

How do I reset my progress?

Open Settings in the app and tap Reset all progress. This deletes your answer history, missed questions, bookmarks and exam history.

Will this app certify me?

No. Section 608 certification is issued only after you pass a proctored exam with an EPA-approved certifying organization. Pass EPA 608 helps you prepare for that exam.

Where do the questions come from?

EPA does not publish its exam questions. The questions are written from the Section 608 regulations (40 CFR Part 82, Subpart F) and the topics covered in common study materials. Rules and figures such as penalty amounts change over time, so check epa.gov/section608 for the latest requirements.
